# 0.1.1 — 2026-10-01

## Added

- **`geonames_search_places`** — search 13M+ places by `query` (`match`: `name_required`, `any_field`, `exact_name`, `name_prefix`) and `countries`, `featureClasses`, `featureCodes`, `cities`, `boundingBox`; `orderBy` relevance or population, `limit` up to 100, `offset` up to 5000 with `nextOffset`. 1 credit, cached an hour.
- **`geonames_get_place`** — the full record for one `geonameId`: admin levels 1–5 with codes and ids, timezone with UTC offsets on 1 January and 1 July ([#1](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/1)), elevations, alternate names (`nameLanguages` filter), postal codes, links, and airport, UN/LOCODE, and Wikidata identifiers.
- **`geonames_get_hierarchy`** — a feature's parent chain from Earth and its continent down to the feature, each level with `geonameId`, feature code, and coordinates.
- **`geonames_get_children`** — a feature's direct children in the `administrative` (default), `tourism`, `geography`, or `dependency` tree. Up to 1,000 children are fetched once and cached, so `nameContains`, `limit`, and `offset` spend no further credit.
- **`geonames_reverse_geocode`** — a coordinate's country and admin levels down to ADM5, or the ocean offshore, plus the nearest populated places or, with `featureClasses`/`featureCodes`, features; `includeTimezone` adds local time, sunrise, and sunset. 1 to 7 credits.
- **`geonames_find_postal_codes`** — `mode` `code`, `place_name`, or `nearby` (`radiusKm` up to 30) across the 122 countries with GeoNames postal data; a mode missing its fields fails as `mode_fields_mismatch`.
- **`geonames_get_countries`** — ISO alpha-2, alpha-3, numeric, and FIPS codes, `geonameId`, capital, population, area, languages, currency, and postal format, by code, `continent`, or `nameContains`; unknown codes land in `notFound`.
- **`geonames_list_reference`** — `topic` `feature_classes` (9) and `feature_codes` (684), bundled and free, or `postal_countries` (122, 1 credit a day).
- **`geonameId` inputs** accept a geonames.org URL and stop at 2147483647, the largest id GeoNames parses ([#2](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/2)). List inputs also take a comma-separated string; codes match in any case, and `UK` reads as `GB`.
- **`GeoNamesService`** — one boundary to `secure.geonames.org`: per-endpoint parameter allowlists, a 20 s retry deadline, per-account pacing at 1,000 requests an hour, single-flight, and a cross-caller cache keyed without the account. GeoNames' absence placeholders (`population: 0`, `geonameId: 0`, `adminCode1: "00"`) are dropped.
- **`GEONAMES_USERNAME`** — the server's GeoNames account, optional; every tool also takes `geonamesUsername` (alias `username`) to spend the caller's own. Account failures are typed (`username_required`, `caller_account_rejected`, `server_account_rejected`, `quota_exhausted`), and the name never reaches logs, output, or cache keys.
- **Data terms** — GeoNames data is CC BY 4.0, and the server instructions tell callers to credit GeoNames when passing results on; community-edited names render as inline data in `content[]`.
