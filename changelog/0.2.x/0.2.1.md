# 0.2.1 — 2026-11-15

## Changed

- **`geonames_reverse_geocode` full-page nearby notice** prints the `geonames_search_places` call with one `boundingBox` whose `west` is above `east` when the radius crosses the 180° meridian, in place of the "box on each side" instruction ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/33)). Boxes that stop short of the meridian, and the poles, print as before.
- **The framework's tool-error trailer** gains a `retry after <value>` term between `retryable` and the request id when the error carries `data.retryAfter` (`@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.19).

## Fixed

- **`geonames_search_places` `boundingBox`** with `west` above `east` is read as crossing the 180° meridian, as GeoNames reads it, and sent unchanged; the Russia, Fiji, and Kiribati country boxes and crossing `geonames_get_place` boxes pass as they stand. Its notice gives the longitude span on every page, including a zero-hit one ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/31)).
- **`invalid_bounding_box`** still rejects `south` at or above `north` and `west` equal to `east` (or `west` 180 with `east` -180) before any request; its recovery hint no longer advises splitting a crossing box ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/31)).
- **A bounding box GeoNames writes as `east: -180`** (New Zealand's country row) is relayed as `180`, and `west: 180` as `-180`, in `geonames_get_countries` and `geonames_get_place` ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/31)).
- **`geonames_get_countries` and `geonames_get_place` `boundingBox` descriptions** no longer call the box the country's mainland, and state that `west` above `east` crosses the 180° meridian ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/31)).
- **`geonames_get_place` Country line** for a record with a continent and no country reads `- **Country:** Not available · continent AS` in `content[]`; `structuredContent` is unchanged ([#32](https://github.com/cyanheads/geonames-mcp-server/issues/32)).
